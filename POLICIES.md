# Policy page starter wording

Four legal pages, ready to paste into the **Policy / Legal** template.

## ⚠ Read this first

**This is a starting point, not legal advice.** It was written for a UK-based
trade wine merchant selling to businesses, and it makes assumptions about how
you operate that may be wrong. Anything in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]` must be filled
in or deleted. Alcohol, duty and B2B distance selling all carry obligations
this cannot know about, and getting a refund or licensing clause wrong on a
wine business is expensive. **Have a solicitor read all four before you publish.**

## How to build each page

1. **Pages → Add New**, title it (e.g. *Privacy Policy*)
2. **Page Attributes → Template → Policy / Legal**
3. Fill in the **Policy** tab: eyebrow, last reviewed date, intro
4. Add one **Section** row per numbered clause below — heading in *Heading*,
   the text in *Body*
5. Assign all four to **Appearance → Menus → Footer — Legal**

Sections are numbered automatically, each gets its own anchor link, and a
contents list appears once there are more than two.

# 1. Privacy Policy

you and your business.

### Who we are

in [ENGLAND AND WALES] under company number [NUMBER], at [REGISTERED ADDRESS].
We are the data controller for the information described in this policy. Our
contact for privacy questions is [PRIVACY EMAIL].

### What we collect
When you apply for a trade account we collect your name, business name,
business address, email address, telephone number, and where relevant your VAT
number and trade references. When you order we also hold your delivery
addresses, order history, and the last four digits and card type of the payment
method — never the full card number, which stays with our payment provider.

We also collect technical information automatically: IP address, browser and
device type, and the pages you visited. See our Cookie Policy for detail.

### Why we use it
- To assess your trade application and verify you are a business
- To take, fulfil, deliver and invoice your orders
- To meet our legal duties on alcohol sales, duty and record keeping
- To answer your questions and manage your account
- To send service messages about your orders and account
- To send trade news and allocation offers, where you have asked us to

### Our lawful bases
Performance of a contract, for anything needed to fulfil an order. Legal
obligation, for records we must keep — including [ALCOHOL WHOLESALER
REGISTRATION SCHEME (AWRS)] and duty records. Legitimate interests, for
verifying businesses, preventing fraud and improving the site. Consent, for
marketing, which you can withdraw at any time.

### Who we share it with
Our delivery partners, so they can deliver; our payment provider, to take
payment; our accountants and HMRC, for tax and duty; and our hosting and email
providers, who process data on our instructions. We never sell your data.

### How long we keep it
Order and duty records for [SIX] years after the end of the relevant financial
year, because we are required to. Unsuccessful applications for [12 MONTHS].
Marketing consent until you withdraw it. Everything else for as long as your
account is open, plus [SIX] years.

### Your rights
You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to
processing, ask us to restrict it, or ask for it to be transferred elsewhere.
Email [PRIVACY EMAIL] and we will reply within one month. You can complain to
the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

### Security
Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is limited to staff who need it. No
system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights we will tell you
and the ICO as the law requires.

### Changes
We will update the "last reviewed" date at the top when this policy changes,
and tell account holders by email if the change is significant.

# 2. Refund Policy

**Intro:** Returns, shortages, breakages and cancellations on trade orders.

### This is a business-to-business policy
We sell to businesses. Consumer distance-selling cancellation rights do not
apply. What follows are the terms we offer trade accounts.
**[CONFIRM WITH YOUR SOLICITOR — this changes if you ever sell to consumers.]**

### Check your delivery on arrival
Please check the number of cases and their condition **before you sign**. Note
any shortage or visible damage on the carrier's paperwork — without that note a
claim is very hard to pursue with the carrier.

### Breakages and shortages
Tell us within **[THREE] working days** of delivery, with the order number and
a photograph of the damage and the outer case. We will replace the wine, or
credit it if we cannot. There is no charge to you for a confirmed breakage or
shortage.

### Faulty wine
Wine that is corked, oxidised or otherwise faulty is replaced or credited.
Tell us within **[30 DAYS]** and keep the bottle with at least [HALF] its
contents — we may need it for our own claim on the producer.

### Wine ordered in error
Unopened, undamaged, still in its original outer case, and reported within
**[SEVEN] days**: we will accept a return, subject to a [RESTOCKING FEE OF
[X]% / COLLECTION CHARGE]. Return carriage is yours unless we shipped the
wrong thing.

### What we cannot take back
Wine that has left temperature-controlled conditions, wine bought on an
allocation or en primeur basis, special orders placed for you specifically, and
anything opened or part-used — except where faulty.

### Cancelling an order
Before it leaves our warehouse, at no charge. Once it has been dispatched it
is a return, as above.

### How to make a claim
Email [ORDERS EMAIL] with your order number, what is wrong, and photographs.
We will respond within [TWO] working days. Approved credits are applied to your
account or refunded to the original payment method within [14] days.
